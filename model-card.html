Antahpragna model card.

A plain account of what the model is, how it was built and tested, and where it falls short.

Task: scam message classification13 Indian languages32 categoriesOn-device, AndroidLast updated: November 2026

Model details

Name
Antahpragna on-device classifier
Architecture
Student model distilled from a 506 million parameter MuRIL teacher. The student has 55.4 million parameters.
Training method
Knowledge distillation with temperature scaling and a combined KL-divergence and cross-entropy loss.
Deployment format
ONNX, INT8 quantised, with NNAPI FP16 acceleration on supported Android devices.
Output
One of 32 scam categories for an input message, with the app’s risk verdict built on top.
Languages
13 Indian languages.

Intended use

In scope

Helping individuals notice likely scam messages in SMS, messaging apps, links and UPI requests, so they can pause before they act.

Out of scope

Legal or forensic decisions, enforcement against individuals, bulk surveillance of messages, or any use that removes human judgement.

Users

Everyday phone users, including families who want extra safeguards for parents and elders.

Evaluation

MeasureResultWhat it means
Validation accuracy99.68%Measured on our held-out validation set.
Pre-release test suite138 casesHand-built cases covering tech-support, delivery and KYC scams and minority-language patterns.
Read the 99.68% carefully.It is a validation score. Real-world accuracy can be lower, especially for new scam patterns and under-represented languages. That is why we keep a separate test suite and publish its findings.

Known limitations

  • Weak spots found in testingTech-support scams, delivery scams and some minority-language patterns were weaker areas in pre-release testing, and KYC scams were sometimes misclassified.
  • False positivesSystem notifications triggered false alarms in testing. We reduced this with a package-name whitelist. Some false positives can still occur.
  • New scamsScammers change wording quickly. A message the model has not seen the pattern of may be missed, so the app is an aid and not a guarantee.

Privacy and data handling

Analysis runs on the user’s own device. Message contents are not sent to our servers. Background scanning uses Android’s notification and accessibility services for this purpose only. See our privacy policy and security page.

We plan to offer the model through a hosted API and a web playground, running in Indian cloud regions. When that launches, this page will describe how that service handles text you submit.
