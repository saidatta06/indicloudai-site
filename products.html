Antahpragna: offline scam detection for Android.

Scams in India arrive as SMS, WhatsApp messages, links and UPI requests, in many languages. Antahpragna analyses them on the phone and warns you before you act.

What it does

  • UPI Risk CheckerChecks a payment request before you pay.
  • Link ScannerFlags suspicious links in messages.
  • Clipboard Auto-ScanChecks copied text for scam patterns.
  • Family Protection ModeExtra safeguards for parents and elders.
  • Emergency ScreenClear next steps if you have already been targeted.

Why on-device

The model runs entirely on the phone. It works without internet, responds instantly, and message contents are not sent to our servers.

Permissions and transparency

Background scanning uses Android’s notification and accessibility services to read incoming messages for scam detection. This analysis happens locally on the device. We use these permissions only for that purpose, and we explain each one in the app before asking for it.
