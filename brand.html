Using the logo

Name
Write it as IndiCloudAI, one word, with capital I, C and AI.
Clear space
Keep a margin around the logo at least as wide as the chakra’s spokes are long.
Minimum size
Use the full logo at 120 px wide or larger. Below that, use the logo mark.
Backgrounds
Use the white-text version on dark backgrounds. Do not place the navy version on dark colours.
Please do not
Stretch, rotate, recolour, add shadows or rearrange the parts of the logo.
