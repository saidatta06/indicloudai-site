Experts, not generalists.

A general-purpose large model tries to answer everything, so it sometimes answers with confidence and no basis. We take the other route: small language models trained on one domain, with sources we can name.

General-purpose LLM

  • Trained on the open internet
  • Can invent facts when unsure
  • Needs heavy, remote infrastructure
  • Data often leaves the country

Domain-expert SLM

  • Trained on curated domain data
  • Answers within its scope, and says when it is outside it
  • Light enough to run in India, even on-premise
  • Data stays where you keep it

What we are building.

Domain language models

Small models for security, public services and regional-language use cases, each built around one field and tested against it.

Sovereign cloud solutions

Architecture and tooling for hosting AI workloads on Indian infrastructure, with identity and access controls designed in from the start.

AI-powered cybersecurity

Detection that works on the device or inside your network, so protection does not depend on sending data abroad.

Three rules we do not bend.

  • Data residency. Training data, prompts and outputs stay in India.
  • No Western dependency. We choose components, hosting and tooling so the product keeps running without them.
  • Honest answers. A model that does not know says so, instead of guessing.
