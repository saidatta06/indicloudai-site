What we do

Domain language models

Compact models trained on one field and tested against it, instead of one model that answers everything.

On-device and India-resident AI

Models run on the user’s device or in Indian cloud regions, so data stays in the country.

AI for cybersecurity

Our first application detects scams in 13 Indian languages, with no internet connection needed.

Data stays in India by design.

Where possible, inference runs on the user’s own device. Any cloud workload runs in Indian regions, so training data, prompts and outputs remain in the country.

Questions, answered.

Does Antahpragna send my messages to a server?

No. The model runs on your phone, and message contents are not sent to our servers.

Why small language models instead of one large model?

A small model trained on one domain can be tested and measured against that domain, and it is light enough to run on a device or in an Indian data centre.

Where does your cloud data live?

Our production design runs any cloud workload in Indian regions, so training data, prompts and outputs remain in India. The public playground preview currently runs on a demo server in Singapore, and we plan to move it.
