Deep technology for India, made in India.

IndiCloudAI is an early-stage startup in Telangana building domain-expert AI that is accurate, private and hosted in India.

Our mission

India needs AI built for its languages, its risks and its data laws. We build small, domain-expert language models that run on devices and in Indian cloud regions, so people and organisations get useful AI without sending sensitive data abroad.

We focus on small models because a model built for one job can be tested, measured and trusted, which general-purpose models struggle to offer.
